PROJECT BACKGROUND

• PinjamGeng is a responsive web-based equipment booking and inventory management application tailored specifically for Universiti Poly-Tech Malaysia (UPTM), built using PHP 8.2, MySQL InnoDB, and Tailwind CSS.

• It allows student club executive committees, event coordinators, and university societies to browse verified audio-visual and IT hardware, view real-time available stock counters, and submit reservation requests 24/7 online.

• The system eliminates reliance on slow paper logbooks, prevents catastrophic double-booking clashes, and establishes end-to-end return accountability across campus facilities.

1

PROBLEM STATEMENTS

1

Manual & Uncentralised Booking Process

Manual, uncentralised processes make verifying live equipment availability and managing records difficult, causing reservation clashes, communication breakdowns, missing records, and time-consuming workflows.

2

Equipment Selection Difficulty & Lack of Packages

Users struggle to identify suitable gear for diverse event types when equipment information and booking records are managed separately; existing systems focus solely on individual items rather than convenient event-based packages.

2

PROJECT OBJECTIVES

1

To Develop an Event Equipment Booking & Inventory Management System

To develop a responsive web-based system enabling users to check availability, submit, and track requests 24/7, while allowing administrators to manage equipment records and booking approvals within the 12-week development period.

2

To Implement an Event-Based Equipment Package Feature

To provide curated, predefined equipment packages for at least four campus event types—including seminars, workshops, meetings, and photography events—within the 12-week development period.

3

AGILE METHODOLOGY

• A methodology whereby work can be broken into small, dynamic, iterative increments called sprints.

• Utilizes an iterative approach in software development, allowing continuous feedback from supervisor Madam Jihadah Binti Ahmad and rapid refinement.

• Executed across 5 two-week sprints: Sprint 1 (Architecture & DB), Sprint 2 (Auth & Inventory), Sprint 3 (Booking Engine & Packages), Sprint 4 (Return & Damage), Sprint 5 (Deployment & UAT).
